## Full description (4000 max)

```
Shh... the cats are napping.

Meowsweeper is a cozy, cat-filled take on the classic minesweeper puzzle. Cats are asleep under the cushions. Tap a cushion to lift it, read the numbers, and work out where every cat is hiding without waking a single one.

EVERY BOARD IS FAIR
No lucky guesses, ever. Every one of the 500 levels can be solved by logic alone. If you are stuck, there is always a clue you have not used yet.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a cushion to lift it.
• A number tells you how many sleeping cats are touching that square.
• Long-press to leave a paw print where you think a cat is napping.
• Clear every safe cushion to win the level.
• Wake a cat and you lose a heart. Three hearts per level.

500 LEVELS, 20 COZY ROOMS
Start on a sunny windowsill with a tiny 5x5 board and work your way through the laundry basket, the bookshelf, the attic, the fish market, the lighthouse and beyond. Boards grow, yarn knots and blocks get in the way, and Hard levels pay double coins.

HELPFUL BOOSTERS
• Sniff: reveals one safe cushion.
• Whisker Scan: peeks under a patch of cushions.
• Catnip: marks every cat in an area for you.

COLLECT CATS AND CUSHIONS
Adopt cats, each with its own perk: an extra heart, cheaper boosters, bonus coins and more. Dress the board in Gingham, Knit, Velvet, Patchwork or Starry cushions.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
Daily tasks, a Rewards Box that fills as you play, and the Bell Quest for players who like a streak.

MADE TO RELAX
• Play with one hand, in portrait.
• No timers on normal levels. Think as long as you like.
• Works offline.
• English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, German and French.

Earn three stars on every level, fill your home with cats, and above all: don't wake the cat.

Tap. Think. Don't wake the cat.
```
